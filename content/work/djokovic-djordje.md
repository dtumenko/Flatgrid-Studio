summary: Djokovic & Djordje is a documentary wedding photography duo based in Valjevo, Serbia, known for their natural, unobtrusive storytelling style. The brand identity redesign refreshed their existing logo and logotype, introduced a warmer, more cohesive color palette and typography system, and extended the new visual identity across print materials — including business cards and wedding albums. The goal was to give their brand the same honesty and warmth found in their photography, turning years of documentary work into an identity that feels just as personal as the stories they capture.
